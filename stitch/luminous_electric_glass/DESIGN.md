## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-clarity technological intelligence combined with radiant optical energy. It captures the precision of modern high-performance interfaces while avoiding utilitarian sterility through the deliberate interplay of deep midnight structure, crystalline frosted glass, and radiant micro-accents.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate Realism** with **Vibrant Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-clean structural foundations in crisp snow and light slate.
- Heavyweight midnight blue typography conveying authority and depth.
- Tactile, glowing dynamic blue interactive triggers.
- Multi-chromatic ambient glows and luminous jewel-toned accents (amber, mint, violet) used systematically to surface state, value, and momentum without clutter.
- Crisp frosted surfaces with sub-pixel borders that elevate the UI into layered optical planes.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast structural darks against clean luminous planes, highlighted by focused spectral energy.

### Core Canvas & Neutrals
- **Background Root**: `#FFFFFF` for primary content canvases.
- **Surface Level 1**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for structural page shells and container underlays.
- **Surface Level 2**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for inset wells, segmented control tracks, and input baselines.
- **Headlines & Text Dark**: `#0B1B4F` (Midnight Deep Navy) provides authoritative optical contrast for high-priority typography.
- **Body & Secondary Copy**: `#334155` (Slate 700) and `#64748B` (Slate 500) for effortless legibility.
- **Sub-Pixel Borders**: `rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.08)` across standard components; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` when cutting across frosted glass.

### Interactive & Accents
- **Primary Action Gradient**: Linear blend from `#3B82F6` (Electric Blue) to `#4F46E5` (Royal Indigo), delivering active energy.
- **Luminous Amber & Gold**: `#FBBF24` for high-value metrics, spotlight chips, and critical attention items.
- **Soft Ember / Orange**: `#F97316` paired with `#FDBA74` / `#FDE68A` for warnings, energetic tags, and warm contrast.
- **Emerald Mint & Cyan**: `#10B981` transitioning to `#06B6D4` for positive indicators, confirmation badges, and telemetry rings.
- **Violet Glow**: `#A855F7` to `#DDD6FE` reserved for luxury attributes, AI triggers, and ambient rim lighting.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a distinction between editorial structure, narrative text, and technical feedback:
- **Headlines & Display (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: High-geometry sans with refined character cuts. Rendered predominantly in `#0B1B4F` with tight negative tracking to maintain punch and clarity at scale.
- **Body & Information (Inter)**: Rational, highly legible neutral workhorse. Applied to long-form reading, documentation, and structural layout panels to maximize scanability.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono)**: Specialized monospaced utility for numerical metrics, tags, currency values, and system badges, grounding the glass aesthetic in technical certainty.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt dynamic fluid grid with a strict 12-column desktop structure scaling into 8 columns on tablet (breakpoint: `768px`) and 4 columns on mobile (breakpoint: `480px`).

- **Outer Margins**: Desktop layouts use generous `3rem` (48px) gutters to maintain air around elevated glass cards; mobile compresses to `1.25rem` (20px) to maximize touch targets.
- **Component Padding Scale**: Internal card paddings default strictly to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile. 
- **Rhythm & Alignment**: Vertical stack intervals strictly follow `space-md` for related elements and `space-xl` for sectional transitions, maintaining an uncluttered, premium cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent glass, ambient blue-tinted shadows, and crisp dual-directional boundary strokes.

### Surfaces & Optical Layers
- **Base Canvas**: Solid `#FFFFFF` or subtle linear gradient from `#FFFFFF` down to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Glass Panel (Standard)**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);`.
- **Glass Panel (Elevated)**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(24px);`.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
- **Level 1 (Card Resting)**: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.04); border: 1px solid rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.06);`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Popovers)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08); border: 1px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.2);`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Overlays)**: `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -8px rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.16), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8);`.
- **Dynamic Glow (Interactive Highlight)**: Soft multi-colored gradient blooms positioned under actionable units using `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25) 0%, rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.15) 50%, transparent 70%)` with a `filter: blur(32px)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded** geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that balances tech precision with soft ergonomic friendliness:
- Standard buttons, input fields, and tags use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Standard cards and floating panels use `1rem` (16px).
- Large dashboard widgets, banners, and modals utilize `1.5rem` (24px).
- Micro-badges, status pills, and toggle indicators apply fully rounded ends (`9999px`) to create optical variety against rectilinear cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #3B82F6 0%, #4F46E5 100%)`), text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `0.5rem`. Subtle dynamic glow on hover with `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`. Transitions are rapid cubic-bezier (`150ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button**: Translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.1)`, text `#0B1B4F`. On hover, background shifts to `#FFFFFF` with primary border accent `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost Button**: Transparent fill, text `#334155`, active hover fill `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Luminous Metric Chip**: JetBrains Mono typography, `padding: 4px 10px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`).
- **Amber Spotlight**: Background `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.12)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.3)`.
- **Mint Success**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Violet Accent**: Background `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.12)`, text `#7E22CE`, border `1px solid rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from standard frosted glass with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and white highlights along the top border (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- Feature clear separation: titles in midnight navy (`#0B1B4F`), metadata in slate (`#64748B`), and active interactive states lifting via subtle translate-Y (`-2px`) and enhanced blue-tinted shadow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Inset fill `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.1)`, height `44px`, text `#0B1B4F`. On focus: solid white background `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#3B82F6`, with focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.18)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Unchecked states have neutral borders `rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.2)`. Checked states immediately take the electric-to-indigo gradient fill with a crisp white icon mark and micro blue glow.

### Lists & Tables
- Alternate row striping using transparent and `#F8FAFC`.
- Dividers are whisper-thin `1px solid rgba(11, 27, 79, 0.05)`.
- Row hover states apply a subtle frosted glass wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) with a left border accent in `#3B82F6`.
